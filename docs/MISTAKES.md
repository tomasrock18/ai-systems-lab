# Mistakes

This file tracks recurring engineering mistake classes, not every small bug.

The goal is to make hidden weaknesses visible and verify that they stop recurring.

No learning-project mistakes have been recorded yet.

---

## Entry template

### Mistake class

**Situation**

What was being implemented?

**Failure mode**

What could or did go wrong?

**Why I missed it**

What assumption or mental model was incorrect?

**Fix**

How was the issue corrected?

**General principle**

What reusable engineering lesson should be carried into future systems?
